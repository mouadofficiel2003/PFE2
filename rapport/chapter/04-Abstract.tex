This end-of-studies project report presents the development of a platform for the dematerialization of application management, designed to digitize the organization of competitive examinations: candidate management, examination centres, establishments and rooms, automatic allocation of candidates, then generation and sending of convocations.

This project was carried out as part of my end-of-studies internship at the Ministry of Economy and Finance, in Rabat. The system aims to assist managers in processing applications and, above all, to automate the assignment of candidates to examination rooms according to available capacities, while making the sending of convocations more reliable and providing an operational overview through a dashboard.

It provides the ministry with an integrated tool that replaces a still largely manual process --- spreadsheets, error-prone allocations and fragmented mailings --- with a single chain, from candidate import through to the convocation. The added value lies in time savings for managers, fewer allocation errors (room capacity, double placement, geographical distance) and more reliable notification of candidates, while retaining human control. To achieve this, the solution was designed and then developed as an internal web application: an interface for ministry staff, a Spring Boot microservices backend and PostgreSQL databases, with automatic allocation according to room capacity and geographical proximity, PDF convocation generation and bulk email sending.

Thus, this abstract places the project within the broader framework of digitizing competitive-examination management and within its specific context of implementation at the Ministry of Economy and Finance, while highlighting its operational value for managers.
